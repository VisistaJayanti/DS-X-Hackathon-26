    Responsible AI & guardrails
    
      
    
        
    Never decides eligibility
    Output says "likely pathway" / "documents support". The verifier strips decision language ("is eligible", "is exempt", "approved"). Packets are drafts until a coordinator ticks the confirmation box.
    
        
    Every claim is cited
    Each sentence carries an exact document quote and/or a rulebook rule with its source URL and verbatim regulatory text. A verifier removes anything unsupported and logs it. The evaluation script reports results with the verifier on and off.
    
        
    Ambiguity is shown, not guessed
    Rules where sources are unclear carry a needs confirmation badge in every view (e.g. medical-frailty letter requirements, postpartum length, "part-time" vs "half-time").
    
        
    What leaves the machine
    Synthetic data only. In live mode, document text is sent only to the API calls of the configured model providers (free preset: Groq, Google Gemini, Cerebras; or Anthropic). Free tiers may retain prompts, which is acceptable only because the data is synthetic. Nothing goes to other third parties; document contents are not logged. Cached responses stay in a git-ignored local folder. Offline mode sends nothing anywhere.
    
        
    Fairness
    Evaluation is broken down by member language and case type, so gaps in performance for limited-English members or specific exemptions are visible (see eval/RESULTS.md).
    
        
    No dark patterns
    Every outreach message tells the member how to contact the agency directly, includes opt-out for texts, never asks for a Social Security number by text, and never pressures or implies a decision.
    
        
    Recover without false promises
    Members dropped for paperwork are told they may be able to get coverage back without a new application, by a dated deadline, and that the agency decides. Nothing says coverage will be reinstated.
    
        
    Neutral routing on transition
    When the evidence suggests someone may no longer qualify, options are listed without preference and the member chooses. Plan contracts may bar steering members to the plan's own marketplace product, so the tool never ranks or favours one. A navigator confirms before anything changes.
    
        
    Risk flags help, never harm
    The risk score is a transparent points list with reasons. It only orders who gets help first. It is never used to deny, delay, or deprioritise coverage or services, and members not flagged can always get help.
    
        
    Human in the loop
    Coordinators and navigators review every packet, letter and message. Machine-drafted translations are labelled and should be reviewed by a native speaker before real use.
